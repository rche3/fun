# Shanghai 上海

September 2026 Trip.

- Hotel: Grand Hyatt Shanghai 上海金茂君悦大酒店 (Jin Mao Tower, Lujiazui)

- Food
    - Region-specific Chinese cuisine
        - Bing Cheng Lao Yu Jia 冰城老于家(徐家汇店)
            - **Description:** North-eastern Chinese food (e.g. guo bao rou, pancake).
            - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
        - Fei Da Chu 费大厨辣椒炒肉(陆家嘴中心店)
            - **Description:** Hunan-style food. Savoury, strong flavours, very tasty.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
        - Four Seasons of People's Blessing 四季民福烤鸭店 (中信泰富店)
            - **Description:** Beijing-style duck.
            - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
    - Hotpot
        - Banu Hotpot 巴奴毛肚火锅(上海陆家嘴中心店)
            - **Description:** Nice hotpot.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
        - Haidilao 海底捞火锅(华山路店)
            - **Description:** The famous hotpot chain.
            - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Shanghai classic restaurants
        - Lao Ji Shi 老吉士酒家(天平路店)
            - **Description:** Classic Shanghainese food.
            - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Bars
        - FLAIR 顶层餐厅酒吧(浦东丽思卡尔顿酒店)
            - **Description:** Best view of the Oriental Pearl Tower / skyline.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
        - Conde (上海海鸥丽晶酒店)
            - **Description:** Good view of the Oriental Pearl Tower / skyline.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
- Malls / Shopping
    - Shanghai Financial Centre 上海国金中心商场 (IFC)
        - **Description:** Big.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
    - Xintiandi 新天地广场
        - **Description:** Old shikumen lanes turned into shops and bars.
        - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
    - Two ITC 上海国贸中心二期
        - **Description:** New mall in Xujiahui.
        - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Grand Gateway 港汇恒隆广场
        - **Description:** Big mall in Xujiahui.
        - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - The Louis (Big LV boat) 路易号
        - **Description:** The big LV boat.
        - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
- Sightseeing
    - North Bund 北外滩
        - **Description:** Riverside walk with skyline views.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
    - Yu Garden 豫园 / 城隍庙
        - **Description:** Classical garden and old-town bazaar.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
    - Wukang Mansion 武康大楼
        - **Description:** The famous wedge-shaped old building.
        - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Jing'an Temple 静安寺
        - **Description:** Golden temple among the skyscrapers.
        - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
    - Lujiazui 陆家嘴
        - **Description:** The skyscrapers: Shanghai Tower, Jin Mao, the Pearl.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)

---

_Note: 老吉士酒家(天平路店) is on Tianping Road, which is French Concession / Xujiahui —
