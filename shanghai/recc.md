# Shanghai

Three zones. Pick one per day and walk it — everything below is clustered so you're
not burning 40 minutes in a Didi between stops.

- **Zone 1 — Wukang Road / French Concession** (leafy, low-rise, plane trees, Xujiahui at the south end)
- **Zone 2 — The Bund / Lujiazui** (the skyline, the tall stuff, the old town)
- **Zone 3 — Jing'an Temple** (Nanjing West Road, malls and temples in the same block)

---

- Food 🥘
    - Region-specific Chinese cuisine
        - Bing Cheng Lao Yu Jia 冰城老于家(徐家汇店)
            - **Description:** North-eastern Chinese style food (e.g., guo bao rou, pancake). Big portions, cheap, loud — order the guo bao rou and a cumin lamb and you're done. Good late.
            - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
        - Fei Da Chu 费大厨辣椒炒肉(陆家嘴中心店)
            - **Description:** Hunan - style food, savory and strong flavours (very tasty). The signature chilli-fried pork is the whole point — get rice, it's salty by design. Queue at peak, so go early or put your name down and walk the mall.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
        - Four Seasons of People's Blessing 四季民福烤鸭店 (中信泰富店)
            - **Description:** Beijing-style duck. The Beijing institution, done properly — crisp skin, carved at the table, sugar on the side. Half a duck is enough for two with a couple of sides. Book ahead.
            - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
    - Hotpot
        - Banu Hotpot 巴奴毛肚火锅(上海陆家嘴中心店)
            - **Description:** Nice hotpot. The tripe (毛肚) is the reason it exists — 7 seconds in, 7 out. Ingredient quality well above the chains, priced accordingly.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
        - Haidilao 海底捞火锅(华山路店)
            - **Description:** The famous one. Food is fine, the service is the experience — free snacks and drinks while you wait, hand-pulled noodle performance, they'll do your nails. Good with a group, open very late.
            - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Shanghai classic restaurants
        - Lao Ji Shi 老吉士酒家(天平路店)
            - **Description:** Proper old-school Shanghainese. Small, cramped, no ceremony. Hongshao rou, drunken chicken, crab-roe tofu, the fried rice. Cash-era vibes in a good way — book, it's tiny.
            - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Bars
        - FLAIR 顶层餐厅酒吧(浦东丽思卡尔顿酒店)
            - **Description:** Best view of oriental pearl TV tower / Shanghai skyline. 58th floor of the Ritz, outdoor terrace pointed straight at the Pearl Tower. Drinks are expensive and that's not the point. Go at sunset, book the terrace.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
        - Conde (上海海鸥丽晶酒店)
            - **Description:** Good view of oriental pearl TV tower / Shanghai skyline. Lower and closer than FLAIR — you're at the river's edge looking across, which honestly frames the skyline better. Much easier to walk into.
            - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
- Malls / Shopping 🛍️
    - Shanghai Financial Centre 上海国金中心商场 (IFC)
        - **Description:** Big. The Lujiazui default — luxury on the lower floors, solid food hall, connected underground to the metro and the towers so you never go outside. Easy rainy-day plan.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
    - Xintiandi 新天地广场
        - **Description:** Restored shikumen lane houses turned into an open-air mall. Touristy, but genuinely nice to walk at night. Come for the bars and people-watching rather than the shopping.
        - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
    - Two ITC 上海国贸中心二期
        - **Description:** The newer, quieter end of Xujiahui. Well-curated, not crowded, good cafés. Pair it with Grand Gateway across the road if you're actually shopping.
        - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Grand Gateway 港汇恒隆广场
        - **Description:** The big Xujiahui workhorse mall — sits right on top of the metro interchange. Everything's here, nothing's special. Good for a top-up run.
        - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - The Louis (Big LV boat) 路易号
        - **Description:** The LV ship on Nanjing West Road — a full-size boat as a store, with a café and exhibition upstairs. Worth it for the photo even if you buy nothing. Book the café slot in advance.
        - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
- Sightseeing 🗺️
    - North Bund 北外滩
        - **Description:** The quieter side of the river with the best single view in the city — Pudong on one side, the old Bund on the other. Go at dusk, walk the promenade.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
    - Yu Garden 豫园 / 城隍庙
        - **Description:** Ming-dynasty garden wrapped in a very busy bazaar. Garden itself is lovely and calm; the surrounding market is chaos. Morning, before the tour groups.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)
    - Wukang Mansion 武康大楼
        - **Description:** The wedge-shaped 1924 building at the fork — the most photographed corner in Shanghai. Fifteen minutes of looking, then walk the side streets, which are the real reason to be here.
        - **Location:** Wukang Road / French Concession
    - Jing'an Temple 静安寺
        - **Description:** Working golden temple sitting in the middle of the shopping district, skyscrapers on all sides. That contrast is the appeal. Quick stop, then straight into the malls.
        - **Location:** Jing'an Temple
    - Lujiazui 陆家嘴
        - **Description:** The three towers — Shanghai Tower, Jin Mao, SWFC — plus the Pearl. Do the sky walk circle between them at night. Shanghai Tower observation deck if it's clear.
        - **Location:** The Bund / Lujiazui (tall central buildings)

---

_Note: 老吉士酒家(天平路店) is on Tianping Road, which is French Concession / Xujiahui —
I moved it out of the Bund zone so the map clusters correctly._
